Our Story & Heritage

Born out of a passion for authentic flavors, community gatherings, and culinary innovation.

Established 2021

From a Small Family Kitchen to Freetown's Favorite Table

Lenosol Foods started with a simple belief: great food brings people together across every walk of life. What began as weekend pop-up grill nights quickly grew into a full-service dining destination.

Every spice blend is roasted in-house, every sauce is simmered from scratch, and every guest is welcomed with genuine warmth.

What Drives Our Kitchen

01. Sustainability

Zero-waste prep practices and biodegradable packaging for all takeaway orders.

02. Local Empowerment

Over 85% of our produce, seafood, and spices are sourced from regional farmers and cooperatives.

03. Culinary Craft

Respecting traditional recipes while embracing modern plating and cooking techniques.
